# Citrate Memories in your browser (nothing to install)

This is the easiest way to use Citrate Memories, and it needs no setup.

1. Go to **https://memrizz.citrate.ai**
2. Sign in with your Citrate account (the same login as the rest of our tools).
   First time with email + password, you'll confirm a 6-digit code we email you —
   that's what verifies your address.
3. You'll see the **constellation** of the federation. Use the search box, or
   click a repo to read its storyline.

That's it. If you can sign in and see the map, you're done.

## What you can do here

- **Search / Ask** — type a question in plain language ("what did we decide about
  the 40204 chain id?"). Answers carry a freshness note and tell you if anything
  has been superseded or contradicted.
- **Fly the constellation** — each node is a memory; click one to read it and see
  what it connects to.

## If sign-in fails

It's almost always an account/permissions thing, not you:

- **Can't sign in at all** → you may not have a Citrate account yet. Ask the team.
- **Signed in but the map is empty / "no access"** → your email isn't on the team
  allowlist yet. Ask an operator to add it (it takes them one line). The moment
  they do, refresh and it's there.

You never need a key or token for this path — access follows your login.
